# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participation should be respectful, constructive and focused on safe, authorized research and engineering.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, precise and open to correction.
- Distinguish observed facts, hypotheses, interpretations and unknowns.
- Respect privacy, authorization boundaries and project scope.
- Report security concerns privately through a published security contact where available; do not post secrets or exploitable target details in public issues.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, doxxing, publishing secrets or private data, encouraging unauthorized access, or intentionally misrepresenting test/security results is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close or remove violating content and may restrict participation when necessary. The repository owner must define the final enforcement contact and appeal process before treating this as a complete governance program.
